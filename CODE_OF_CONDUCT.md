# Code of Conduct

Arc Light is a small project. These rules keep it a good place to work.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the code, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and expect to hear why.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Sharing someone else's private information.
- **Posting explicit material anywhere in the project.** That includes issues,
  pull requests, comments, screenshots and test data. The app organises adult
  media, but the project's spaces stay safe for work. Use placeholder content.
- Content involving minors, or anyone who hasn't consented, in any form. This
  leads to an immediate permanent ban and a report to the relevant authorities.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may block anyone who breaks
these rules. To report a problem privately, use the repository's **Security →
Report a vulnerability** form and say it's a conduct report.
